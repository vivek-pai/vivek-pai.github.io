Developed as the capstone project for Carnegie Mellon's Master of Human-Computer Interaction program in partnership with NASA's Johnson Space Center, Parallax is an augmented reality system designed to help astronauts execute complex maintenance procedures aboard the International Space Station.

Working in microgravity with bulky gloves, poor lighting, and no internet connection creates unique challenges for procedure-following. Parallax overlays step-by-step guidance directly onto the physical environment, reducing cognitive load and error rates in high-stakes situations.
