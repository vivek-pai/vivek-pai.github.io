Senior honors thesis exploring whether a computing system that detects and responds to a user's emotional state can measurably improve task performance. The system used physiological signals and facial expression analysis to detect affect, then adapted interface parameters accordingly.

The work was later published at the 27th International Florida Artificial Intelligence Research Society (FLAIRS) Conference.
